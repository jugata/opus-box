# OpusBox User Guide

A listening journal for classical music — browse composers, works, and
recordings, and log what you listen to.

## Composers

**Browse:** `/composers` lists every composer currently in OpusBox.

**Find someone not listed yet:** Use the search box at the top of the
Composers page. Matches already in OpusBox show under "Already in OpusBox" —
click to go straight to their page. Matches found on MusicBrainz but not yet
added show under "Found on MusicBrainz" with a short description to help
you pick the right person (useful when a name is shared by several people,
e.g. "Bach"). Click **Add** to import them — this only adds the composer
themselves; their works aren't pulled in automatically.

## Works

**On a composer's page:** Works already added show as a grid of cards —
click one to see its recordings.

**Find a work not yet added:** Use the search box on the composer's page.
It only searches works by *that* composer. Matches already added show under
"Already in OpusBox"; matches found on MusicBrainz show under "Found on
MusicBrainz" with an **Add** button. Importing a work also pulls in any
recordings MusicBrainz has for it.

There's no bulk "add all works" — you search for and add works one at a
time, as you need them.

## Recordings

**On a work's page:** Recordings show as a list — conductor and orchestra
(when known) and the year, if available. Click one to see its full details
(label, duration, etc.) and log a listening session.

## Logging a listening session

On a recording's page, if you're logged in, click **Log listen**. You can
optionally rate it (1–5) and add notes, then save.

## Your journal

`/journal` shows everything you've logged, newest first — work title,
composer, conductor/orchestra, your rating and notes. From here you can
edit a session's rating/notes or remove it entirely.

## Account

- **Register:** `/register` — email, username, password.
- **Log in:** `/login`.
- Once logged in, the navbar shows a link to your Journal and a log-out
  button.
